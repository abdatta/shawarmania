This website collects nothing

shawarmania.in sets no cookies, runs no analytics, carries no advertising or tracking scripts, and has no accounts or logins. Fonts and photographs are served from our own domain, so loading a page here does not tell anybody else that you did. There is nothing to opt out of, because nothing is switched on.

What we collect at the counter

Everything below happens in person, at our counter, when you buy something.

  • Your mobile number — only if you give it to us, and only so we can send you your bill or recognise you when you come back. Nothing on the menu costs more or less depending on whether you do.
  • Your name — optional, and only so the counter can greet a regular properly.
  • The bill — what you ordered, the prices at that moment, any discount, the total, how you paid, the date and time, and which outlet served you.

We do not ask for, and do not store, your home address, your email, your date of birth, or your card details. A card or UPI payment is settled by your own bank or payment app — we record that an amount was paid and by which method, not your account.

What we use it for

  • Sending you your own bill, on your phone, if you asked for it.
  • Recognising a returning customer at the counter, so we are not asking for the same details again.
  • Running the business — accounts, stock, tax, and knowing which of our own outlets sold what.

That is the whole list. We do not build advertising profiles, we do not score you, and we do not use your number to reach you about anything other than your own orders unless you have separately asked us to.

Your bill on your phone

If you gave us your number, we can send you a link to your own bill. How that programme works, and how to stop it, has its own page: How we message you.

A receipt link is built to be readable only by the person holding it:

  • The address contains a long random token. It cannot be guessed, and changing a digit does not show you somebody else's bill.
  • The page names nobody — not your name, not your number, not even the last four digits of it. Anyone you forward it to sees one order and nothing about you.
  • Search engines are instructed not to index it, and it is not listed in our sitemap.
  • When a receipt link is opened, we record the time, the network address it was opened from and the browser's own identifier. That record exists to spot somebody hunting for other people's bills, and for no other purpose.

We do not sell your number

Your mobile number and your consent to be messaged are never sold, rented, shared or disclosed to any third party for that party's own marketing, and never transferred as part of anyone else's contact list.

Who else handles it, and why

A few services process this information on our instruction, only to do the job we hired them for, and with no right to use it for their own purposes:

  • Our messaging provider — delivers the message carrying your bill link to your phone.
  • Our hosting and database providers — store the bill and serve the receipt page.

We will tell you which companies these are if you ask — {{brand.email}}. Nobody else receives your number.

Ordering through Swiggy or Zomato

If you order through a delivery platform, that order is theirs: they take your details, their app messages you, and their privacy policy governs it, not this one. We receive the order and what is needed to cook and hand it over. Nothing on this page changes what a platform does with your data.

How long we keep it

Bills stay in our accounts for as long as the law requires us to keep our books. Your number and name stay in our customer records while you are a customer of ours.

Ask us and we will remove them. Write to {{brand.email}} or call {{brand.phonePrimary}}. The sale itself stays in our accounts — we cannot unmake a sale that happened — but it stops being connected to you, and we stop being able to message you.

Seeing or correcting what we hold

Ask us what we have about you, ask us to fix it if it is wrong, or ask us to delete it, at {{brand.email}} or {{brand.phonePrimary}}. It is a small business and a short list — you will get a real answer from a person.
