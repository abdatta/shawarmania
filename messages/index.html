How you opted in

There is one way, and it happens in person. Providing your mobile number at our counter opts you into an automatic receipt SMS for that purchase. The same number identifies your points and gold at the outlet. There is no separate messaging checkbox or confirmation.

“Your number gets your receipt and points by SMS. One message per bill, no offers.”

Nobody is added any other way. We do not buy numbers, we do not take them from delivery platforms, and there is no box on this website to tick — because consent given on a website would not be the consent we actually rely on. A number is entered at the counter only because the person standing there gave it.

If you do not want a receipt SMS for a purchase, skip providing your number for that bill. You can still buy normally and ask the counter to show your receipt. Points need your number.

What arrives

  • A link to your own bill — what you ordered, what it cost, how you paid. The page it opens names nobody, so it is safe to keep or forward.
  • Your points — what this bill earned and the balance it left at this outlet.

No offers, no promotions, no marketing. This programme is transactional and that is all it is. If we ever want to send you an offer, we will ask you separately, and saying no to that will not stop your bills arriving.

How often, and what it costs

Roughly one message per bill — buy once, get one message. We charge you nothing. Message and data rates may apply on your own mobile plan.

Help with your receipt

Call {{brand.phonePrimary}} or email {{brand.email}} if your receipt is missing or incorrect. Bills arrive by SMS from the sender name DEDTTL; replying to one does not reach us.

There is no separate SMS subscription or messaging preference. A receipt is sent only for a paid bill carrying your number. Giving your number on a later purchase gets that bill's receipt too. To request removal of your customer details, see our privacy notice; removal also ends points and gold.

Links already sent keep working unless you ask us to revoke a particular receipt link.

If you are getting messages and never gave us your number

Then something is wrong and we want to know. Call {{brand.phonePrimary}} or write to {{brand.email}} and we will find out how the number got there and remove it.
