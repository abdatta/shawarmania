How you opted in

There is one way, and it happens in person. When you pay at our counter, we ask whether you want your bill on your phone. If you say yes, you give the cashier your mobile number, and that is the consent — for that purpose, in those words:

“Want your bill on your phone? Give us your mobile number. One message per bill, no offers. Reply STOP any time.”

Nobody is added any other way. We do not buy numbers, we do not take them from delivery platforms, and there is no box on this website to tick — because consent given on a website would not be the consent we actually rely on. A number is entered at the counter only because the person standing there gave it.

Saying no costs you nothing. The price is the same, the paper bill is the same, and the counter will not ask twice.

What arrives

  • A link to your own bill — what you ordered, what it cost, how you paid. The page it opens names nobody, so it is safe to keep or forward.
  • Occasionally, something about that same order — if we got it wrong and need to tell you.

No offers, no promotions, no marketing. This programme is transactional and that is all it is. If we ever want to send you an offer, we will ask you separately, and saying no to that will not stop your bills arriving.

How often, and what it costs

Roughly one message per bill — buy once, get one message. We charge you nothing. Message and data rates may apply on your own mobile plan.

How to stop

Any one of these is enough. You do not have to explain why.

  • Tell the counter next time you are in. Fastest, and it is done while you watch.
  • Call us on {{brand.phonePrimary}}.
  • Email {{brand.email}} — just the number you want removed is enough.
  • Reply STOP to any message we send you.
  • Call {{brand.phonePrimary}}
  • Email {{brand.email}}

What happens after you stop

  • We stop sending. No confirmation campaign, no “are you sure”, no win-back message.
  • Your number comes out of our customer records, so the counter cannot message you again by accident.
  • You still get a paper bill at the counter, as always, and we can always show you a past bill if you ask.
  • Links we already sent keep working. If you want one killed, tell us and we will revoke it.

If you are getting messages and never gave us your number

Then something is wrong and we want to know. Call {{brand.phonePrimary}} or write to {{brand.email}} and we will find out how the number got there and remove it.
